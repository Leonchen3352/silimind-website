AI TEAM

Your AI Team

Sili, the leader, commands the Four Masters — 36 digital employees in total.

They each have a name and a signature skill: one runs research, one runs diagnosis, one rides along on client visits, one lays out your formations. Say the task out loud, and they get to work — but the final hammer is always yours.

Why “a team with names and faces”

①

A name is memorable

A number has no warmth; a name does. Hand a task to “Scout” and you remember what he can do — and how far he can help.

②

Clear division of labor

Research goes to Scout, diagnosis to Medic, ride-alongs to Mercury, formations to Daedalus; the leader Sili keeps the whole picture. You never wonder who does what — just say what needs to be done.

③

The final call stays with you

They produce drafts, suggestions, and diagnoses — never overstepping. You edit the drafts, you write the prescriptions, you sign the deals.

THE LEADER SILI
Sili
The Leader · Company Brain

She remembers everything about your business and organizes it into one picture you can read at a glance.

Your client’s preferences, last quarter’s quote, the deal stuck for three days — she keeps them all for you. Ask her “how is this month” before work and she turns the funnel, team performance, and at-risk deals into a list you can read in 30 seconds; which clients to revisit, which deals to push — her suggestion list refreshes every hour.
She does exactly two things: sort the facts, and lay out the suggestions. The decision is yours.

The Four Masters

The Four Masters at the front

Real names live in the product; the aliases live in the story.

神行
MERCURY
Mercury · Sales Companion

A swift courier who covers eight hundred li a day — not a single letter late.

拜访录音一键上传，自动转成结构化纪要，直接进客户时间线；跟进邮件替你拟好草稿；第二天见谁，会前准备连对方上句话都替你翻出来。线索评分、商机五维诊断、每日健康分、异常巡检，他一路随行。

He rides along but never leads: you edit the drafts, you set the quote, you sign the deal.
百晓生
SCOUT
Scout · Research Analyst

Master of the weapons ledger — every piece of industry intelligence, in one book.

Drafts outreach emails by persona and runs the compliance check first; scores every new lead so you can see at a glance whether it’s worth pursuing; enter a company domain and he finds the contact’s email, with title and confidence. Usage and spend, fully transparent.

He packs the intelligence; whether to strike is your call.
平一指
MEDIC
Medic · Deal Doctor

One finger on the pulse — alive or dying, he can tell at a touch.

Five-dimension diagnosis shows where the deal is stuck; hourly patrols across the full pipeline, anomalies flagged on the spot; the daily health score arrives on time every morning. His finest art: which piece of content brought which deal — traced from first read to signature, one line all the way.

He diagnoses; you write the prescription.
黄药师
DAEDALUS
Daedalus · Systems Architect

The entire Peach Blossom Isle is his work: formations, mechanisms, every blade of grass placed by design.

Say what you need and he builds your own digital employee: persona, visible data (read-only), attached skills — an array laid out by your decision logic. Every capability comes with a full function card: what it does, how it’s billed, where the boundaries are — one page of answers, on demand.

He lays the formation; you walk the path until it’s familiar.

How to put them to work

Door 1

Conversational summoning

Talk in plain language in the chat box: “draft a follow-up email for Zhang”, “where is this deal stuck”, “what are the opportunity keywords in this industry” — the task routes itself to the right hand.

Door 2

Connect your channels

WeChat, WeCom, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, email — connect them through the integration center; whichever channel a customer comes from, the timeline keeps up.

Door 3

Usage counted per credit

See what AI spend looks like by module: every query, every email, every note — itemized and checkable.

Will they make decisions for me?

不会。他们出手稿、出建议、出诊断，The final call stays with you：草稿你改，方子你开，单子你签。

What if they make a mistake?

Every action leaves a trail: sensitive operations run approval-first, and change records are traceable all the way.

Is my data safe?

One system serves multiple companies with row-level isolation — data is invisible across tenants; enterprise plans support private deployment on demand, keeping data in your own hands.
