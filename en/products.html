🧹 Deduplication & one-click merge

The same customer entered as both "Nine" and "Nine Robotics"? The system finds suspected duplicate companies by name similarity, then matches duplicate contacts within a company by name and email. Confirm the merge and interactions, opportunities, tasks, visit records, and contract templates all migrate with the master record — no orphans left. Select multiple duplicate pairs for batch merging with per-pair success/failure results; data cleansing done in one pass.

  • Smart similarity detection: weighted company-name similarity matching — even short names contained in long ones ("Nine" → "Nine Robotics") are recognized
  • Merge means migration: contacts, opportunities, interactions, tasks, tags, visit reports, and contract templates all migrate with the master record — with a change log

Opportunity Kanban

Five stages (new inquiry → in follow-up → quoting → won → lost), advanced by drag and drop. Each card shows only what the decision needs: customer, value, days since last touch (>3 days amber, >7 days red); wins/losses auto-record time and loss reason, and AI reads and writes win probability directly. Click into the detail page for the conversation timeline plus an AI sidebar (auto-extracted core needs, budget range, suggested next step).

Customer 360° Profile

Three tab views: "Sales touchpoints" shows the timeline of all channel messages (with sentiment tags); "Marketing touchpoints" shows content interactions before the lead converted; "AI insights" provides a composite profile with risk flags. An opportunity health score (0-100) is embedded in the profile: auto-calculated across five dimensions — contact frequency, sentiment trend, stage velocity, inbound/outbound ratio, and value — green healthy / yellow watch / red alert.
