ChatBI Smart Q&A

Query data in natural language; charts auto-generated

ChatBI is SiliMind's full-screen AI Q&A interface — like a ChatGPT conversation, but with access to all your CRM business data.

Natural-language queries

No need to learn SQL or understand the database schema. Just ask:

  • "Which channel had the highest deal value last month?" → bar chart auto-generated
  • "Compare Zhang's and Li's conversion rates this month" → comparison trend lines
  • "Which opportunities haven't been touched in 3+ days this week?" → opportunity list
  • "What's the average deal size difference between LinkedIn and WhatsApp channels?" → comparison chart

Export-Ready

Embedded Mode

Management Dashboard

Real-time metrics + AI alerts — decisions no longer run on gut feeling

The management dashboard is the exclusive home page for admins/bosses — completely different from what sales and marketing see.

Core Metrics

Monthly revenue, funnel conversion rates, marketing spend vs. ROI (enter platform/channel/content costs and it auto-computes deal value ÷ spend), total leads and deals. Each card shows month-over-month trends in color.

Team Leaderboard

A bar chart sorted by monthly deal value. Hover shows each rep's opportunity count, average deal size, and conversion rate.

Sales Funnel

Leads → opportunities → quotes → deals, with counts and conversion rates vs. the same period last month.

Channel Performance

A pie chart of lead source share (SEO articles / social / WhatsApp / WeChat / website inquiries / trade shows / referrals) and each channel's lead-to-deal conversion rate.

AI Alerts

Items needing management attention: "3 high-value opportunities untouched for 7+ days", "Lead volume down 20% month-over-month", "A rep's response speed below the team average". AI doesn't wait for managers to ask — it pushes proactively.

Transparent AI Usage

Token consumption, call counts, average latency, and model mix tracked across 8 AI feature categories (suggestion center / contracts / message analysis / visit notes / knowledge base / topics / content creation / ChatBI), so you can see exactly where AI spend goes (costs are self-calculated at each model's pricing).

Opportunity Health Score

Auto-scored across 5 dimensions; risks surface early

Every opportunity gets an automatic 0-100 health score, combining 5 dimensions:

  • Contact frequency — recent frequency vs. historical average
  • Sentiment trend — is communication getting more positive or negative
  • Stage velocity — dwell time in the current stage vs. historical average
  • Inbound ratio — customer-initiated vs. rep-initiated contact
  • Value weighting — opportunity value vs. the team average

Color Coding

Green (70-100) healthy, yellow (40-69) needs attention, red (0-39) alert. Opportunities below 60 automatically trigger AI opportunity diagnosis — the main issues (customer silence / price objections / competitor mentions, 8 categories), suggested actions, and evidence; stalled opportunities also appear as AI suggestion cards on the sales home page.
