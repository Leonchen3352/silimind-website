FAQ

You might be wondering

How is SiliMind different from traditional CRM? +

Traditional CRMs (like HubSpot, Zoho) only manage sales-stage data and sit apart from marketing tools. SiliMind is an AI-native all-in-one platform — leads from content acquisition flow into the CRM automatically, traceable from "which article" to "which customer" to "which deal".

Will SiliMind's AI replace sales reps? +

No. SiliMind's AI is semi-automatic — it analyzes data, generates notes, and suggests next actions, but every outbound communication is confirmed by the rep before sending. AI is the assistant, not the replacement.

Which channels can be managed in one place? +

Automatic collection supports 6 channels: WhatsApp, Email, Telegram, Instagram, Messenger, LinkedIn. The customer timeline supports 10 interaction channel types (including phone calls, memos, personal WeChat, and WeCom — WeChat messages are currently logged manually). WeCom/Feishu account binding is live; automatic WeChat message collection is being evaluated per platform policy. All channel messages converge on the customer 360° profile timeline — no more switching between apps.

Is private deployment supported? +

Yes. SiliMind supports both cloud setup and private deployment (PostgreSQL + Redis) with strict multi-tenant isolation — each company sees only its own data. Personal trials run in the cloud; enterprise plans can add private deployment as needed.

Personal trial vs enterprise plan — what's the difference? +

The personal trial is built for starting solo — a 10M token free grant, billed by actual token usage, including the customer 360° view, AI-written emails and visit notes, and message aggregation. The enterprise plan serves teams and organizations: full role-permission matrix, management dashboard with alerts, row-level isolation and audit, full audit logging, with private deployment as needed — tailored to team size, contact sales for the enterprise plan.

How soon can I use it after registering? +

After you submit the application, review usually completes within 1 business day and your account and initial password are sent to your email — access begins once approved. Users with an invitation code can create an account directly via the invite-code registration channel.

Is the AI proactive acquisition feature compliant? +

Yes. We only use public data (public LinkedIn profiles, Google, public websites), label the source on every search result, include unsubscribe links in outreach, apply frequency limits, and automatically prompt a GDPR compliance check for EU-based targets.

What company size is this for? +

SiliMind primarily serves B2B companies with 5-200 person teams, including foreign trade / cross-border commerce teams, domestic key-account sales teams, and SaaS companies that need content acquisition plus sales management. Both SaaS multi-tenant and private deployment are supported, flexibly fitting different sizes.
